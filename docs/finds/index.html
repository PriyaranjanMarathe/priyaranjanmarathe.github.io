The Two Popes

Saved 2026-09-20

"The Two Popes" - We watched "The Two Popes" https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Two_Popes over the weekend. We have been exploring the way to watch a movie like a TV episode. In bits of 20-30 minutes per day. That gives us the time to talk and discuss the subject matter and doesn't keep us stuck to the sofa for long. I think it also helps to recally the story. What I loved about the movie is the expression on the Pope's face. It shows the power of words, and brilliance of the actors. Also depitcts the maturity of the Western Civilization to allow screening of the movie, without much tamasha. It is definately an indication of a mature tradition of "Wade Wade Jayate Tatwabodha". https://priyaranjanmarathe.substack.com/p/wade-wade-jayate-tatwabodha?
